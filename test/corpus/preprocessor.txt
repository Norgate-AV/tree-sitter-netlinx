Conditional Directives
==================

#ELSE
CONSTANT INTEGER debug_level
#END_IF

---

    (name
      (identifier))
    (local_variable_declaration
      (storage
        (storage_class_specifier))
      (type
        (type_specifier
          (intrinsic_type)))
      (name
        (identifier)))
    (preproc_else
      (constant_definition
        (type_qualifier)
        (type_specifier
          (intrinsic_type))
        (identifier)))))
